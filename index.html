Birthday letter se — wahi baatein, thoda saaf karke.

My Gucchu,

Bahut pyaar karta hoon tumse, dil se. Tum meri sukoon ho. Tumhe lipat ke meko sukoon aata.

Tum baahon mein rehti ho to lagta hai sab jeet lenge hum. Koi bhi pareshaani saath mein dekh lenge.

Tumhare liye bahut protective hoon. Jab tum mere se door hoti ho, thoda darr lagta hai — papa jaisa wala.

Ab bas tumhare liye jeena chahta hoon. Peeche jo galat hua, maan leta hoon. Ab meri life mein bas tum ho.

Tumhare liye hasta hun, tumhare liye rota bhi. Meri zindagi jeene ki wajah tum ho.

Tumhari khushi ke liye sab kar dunga. Promise — maine tumhe kisi cheez ki kami nahi hone dunga. Jo kar sakta hun, sab karunga.

Mera gulgul, mera gobi, mera guggu — tum meri support ho, hasne ki wajah ho. I love you.

Happy birthday meri princess. Hamesha princess ki tarah pyaar karunga, mera baby.

Tumhari success ke liye apni se zyada dua karta rahunga. Grow karo, badho — main side mein hoon.

Main tumhara husband hoon, aur tumhare papa jaisa protective bhi.

Mujhe ladki chahiye par wish karunga beta ho — bas tum khush raho. Tumhari khushi ke liye meri saari kurbaani.

Jaldi shaadi kar lo, draamebaaz! Pehle thoda bura kiya ho to chup chaap sunn leta hoon — paap ka prayschit samajh ke.

Love you mela doll. Muaah.
