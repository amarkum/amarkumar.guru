A love letter

For my wife, Gobi

Every day with you feels like the softest part of the internet — warm, silly, and entirely ours.

Read my letter

Dear Gobi,

I made this little corner of the internet just for you. Not because the world needed another webpage — but because you deserve something made with love, the kind that takes time and care and a whole lot of heart.

You are my favorite person to share quiet mornings with, loud laughter with, and everything in between. This is my way of saying what I sometimes forget to say out loud: thank you for choosing me, again and again.

Why you

You make ordinary Tuesdays feel like something worth celebrating.

Your laugh is my favorite notification.

Home isn't a place — it's wherever Gobi is.

You turn the smallest moments into the ones I remember forever.

Everyday magic

It's the way we cook together and steal bites before dinner is ready. The way you find comfort in my presence and I find mine in yours. The inside jokes that only we understand, the late-night talks, the sleepy good mornings — all of it woven into the life we've built.

I don't need grand gestures to know how lucky I am. I see it in the ordinary, beautiful, perfectly-us moments we share every single day.

Thank you for being my person.

I love you, Gobi.

— Amar
