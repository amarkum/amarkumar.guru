Seven vows

Seven circles around the sacred fire, seven promises for a lifetime.

धर्मे च अर्थे च कामे च नातिचरामि॥

In duty, in prosperity and in love, I shall never go against you. Spoken thrice by the groom at the saptapadi.

  1. १

    Nourishment

    To provide for and care for each other.

  2. २

    Strength

    Of body, mind and spirit, for one another.

  3. ३

    Prosperity

    To build our home with honesty and hard work.

  4. ४

    Happiness

    To share love, respect and harmony with our families.

  5. ५

    Family

    To cherish the generations that come after us.

  6. ६

    Health

    To stand together through every season of life.

  7. ७

    Friendship

    To be companions, now and for all lifetimes.

जानकारीGood to know

Plan your trip

A few notes to help you arrive relaxed, dress for every moment and make the most of a weekend in the hills.

i

Getting there

  • By roadAbout 5–6 hours from Delhi via Moradabad and Kashipur.
  • By trainRamnagar station is a short drive away; Kathgodam is the other option.
  • By airPantnagar is the nearest airport, roughly 2 hours by car.

ii

February weather

Crisp, sunny days and cold nights in the Kumaon foothills. Pack a warm shawl or jacket for the evening celebrations under the stars.

iii

What to wear

  • HaldiSunny yellows and florals you don't mind getting a little turmeric on.
  • MehndiGreens and pastels; keep sleeves easy to roll up for the henna.
  • SangeetYour most festive, dance-ready glam.
  • Shubh VivahTraditional Indian wear in soft pastels and gold.

iv

Around Corbett

  • Jungle safariJim Corbett National Park, India's oldest, is next door.
  • Garjia DeviA temple perched on a rock in the Kosi river.
  • Corbett FallsA peaceful waterfall walk near Kaladhungi.

आपका ठहरावYour stay

Stay with us, both nights

Rooms at Sterling Quinta are set aside for our family and friends for the nights of 5 and 6 February, so you can wake up on the lawns and never miss a moment. Let us know your travel plans and we'll have a room waiting.

Breakfast is on us each morning, cars will shuttle between the farmstay and Ramnagar station, and there'll be chai on the lawn from sunrise. Bring warm layers for the evenings, dancing shoes for the nights, and nothing else but yourselves.
