सात फेरे · Saat Phere

Seven vows

Seven circles around the sacred fire, seven promises for a lifetime.

  1. १

    Nourishment

    To provide for and care for each other.

  2. २

    Strength

    Of body, mind and spirit, for one another.

  3. ३

    Prosperity

    To build our home with honesty and hard work.

  4. ४

    Happiness

    To share love, respect and harmony with our families.

  5. ५

    Family

    To cherish the generations that come after us.

  6. ६

    Health

    To stand together through every season of life.

  7. ७

    Friendship

    To be companions, now and for all lifetimes.
