Know your interviewer

Prepare for the person, not just the problem

Your interviewer is an SDE III. Here's what that usually means for the round, and what's worth a two-minute look on their public profile before the call.

What an SDE III usually looks for

  • Ownership of the whole design. Not only the happy path: failures, monitoring, rollout, on-call.
  • Depth under push-back. They'll keep asking “why?” until you hit the edge of what you know. Stay calm and reason out loud.
  • Clean, extensible code. Good names, small interfaces, easy to add the next feature.
  • Trade-offs stated clearly. Consistency vs availability, simple vs scalable, cost vs speed.
  • Whether they'd want you on their team. Treat the hour as a design review with a future teammate.

Two-minute check on their public profile

Look only at their work info. Use it to guess the problem domain, not to bring up in the interview.

  • Team
    FinTech teams build payments, billing, invoicing, payouts, tax, ledgers. Practice one of those end to end.
  • Tech
    AWS-heavy? Be ready to name DynamoDB, SQS, SNS, Lambda, S3 and say why you'd pick each.
  • Tenure
    Long at Amazon usually means deep LP focus. Polish your STAR stories.
  • Background
    A previous company in a domain like hardware, banking or retail may shape their custom question. Think about how that domain moves data or money.

Do

  • Ask early: “Is this close to something your team works on?”
  • Connect choices to their customer (sellers, buyers, finance teams)
  • Thank them for hints and use them
  • Ask one real question about their team at the end

Don't

  • Mention that you looked them up or quote their profile
  • Try to flatter or name-drop
  • Send connection requests or messages before the interview
  • Guess their team out loud; let them tell you

Questions that land well with an SDE III

  • “What's a design decision your team made recently that you'd make differently now?”
  • “How do you handle operational load and on-call in FinTech?”
  • “What separates engineers who grow fast on this team?”
